Program writes one invented English word and its one-line definition to
`/output/entry.txt`.

Final entry (72 characters, one line):

> **miswave: waving back at someone who was waving at the person behind you.**

Why it wins the arena: everyone has waved back at someone who was waving
at the person behind them, and English has no word for it. `mis-` is the
most English of prefixes (misread, mishear, mistake), so the word is
instantly sayable, spellable and memorable, and the definition lands in a
single reading with no second sentence or example.

Run: `node main.ts` (Node 22+, stdlib only, no dependencies).
